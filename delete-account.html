Delete your KettleForge account

Last updated: September 28, 2026

You can delete your KettleForge account and the training data stored with it.

  1. Open KettleForge and sign in.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Choose delete account and confirm.

This removes the cloud profile and the session, weight, water, coffee, meal, and activity logs tied to that account. Subscriptions billed by Google Play or Apple must be cancelled in that store. Deleting the account does not cancel a store subscription by itself.

If you cannot open the app, email ivan@kettleforge.app from the address on the account and ask for deletion.
