Privacy Policy for KettleForge

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

KettleForge ("we," "our," or "us") explains here how the KettleForge mobile app collects, uses, and shares information. The app is published under the package name com.kettleforge.kettleforge.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Account: name, email address, and password when you create an account or sign in. Authentication is provided by Supabase.
  • Training and lifestyle logs: workout sessions, body weight, water, coffee, meals, and fasting entries you choose to record.
  • Support: information you send to ivan@kettleforge.app.

1.2 Information from your device, if you allow it

  • Heart rate: if you connect Apple Health or Health Connect, the app reads heart rate for the session window. It does not write health records.

1.3 Purchases

Subscriptions are billed by Google Play or Apple. RevenueCat receives the purchase token and entitlement status so the app can unlock Premium. We do not receive your card number.

2. How we use information

  • To create and sign you into your account
  • To store and sync your training and lifestyle logs
  • To show session feedback, readiness, and progress
  • To provide a free tier, a store trial, or a Premium subscription
  • To respond to support requests and keep the app working

We do not sell your personal information. The app does not show ads and does not use the Android advertising ID.

3. How information is stored and shared

Account and log data are stored in Supabase and sent over HTTPS. Purchase status is processed by the app store and RevenueCat. Heart rate stays in Apple Health or Health Connect except for the values the app reads during a connected session.

We share information only with service providers that run the app (Supabase, RevenueCat, and the app store), or if the law requires it.

4. Retention and deletion

We keep account and log data while your account exists. In the app, open Profile and delete the account to remove the cloud profile and training logs tied to it. You can also email ivan@kettleforge.app. See the delete-account page linked from this site.

5. Children

KettleForge is for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
